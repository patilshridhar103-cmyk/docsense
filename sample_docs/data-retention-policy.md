# Data Retention Policy (SAMPLE — fictional)

Document ID: DR-POL-002
Standard: GDPR (illustrative, not official text)

## DR-1 Customer data retention
Customer personal data must be deleted within 30 days of a verified deletion request.

## DR-2 Backup retention
Encrypted backups are kept for 12 months, then permanently destroyed.

## DR-3 Audit logs
Security audit logs must be retained for a minimum of 7 years for compliance purposes.

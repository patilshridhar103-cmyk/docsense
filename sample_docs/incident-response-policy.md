# Incident Response Policy (SAMPLE — fictional)

Document ID: IR-POL-003
Standard: SOC 2 (illustrative, not official text)

## IR-1 Reporting a breach
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery.

## IR-2 Customer notification
Affected customers must be notified within 72 hours of a confirmed data breach.

## IR-3 Post-incident review
A written post-incident review must be completed within 5 business days of resolving an
incident.
